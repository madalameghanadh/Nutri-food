Recommendation Methodology Explained

1. Content-Based Recommendation

The algorithm compares individual food item attributes (calories, protein, fiber, glycemic index, tags) directly against the user's health goal (e.g. Muscle Support vs Weight Management).

2. Rule-Based Penalty Filtering

Foods conflicting with registered food allergies (e.g. dairy, nuts, gluten) or strict dietary boundaries (e.g. Vegan, Vegetarian) trigger an immediate penalty deduction (-100 points) to guarantee safe food discovery.

3. Weighted Mathematical Scoring

Score = (Diet Match × 25) + (Goal Match × 25) + (Preference Match × 20) + (Nutrition Match × 20) + (Cuisine Match × 10) - Penalty

4. Explainable AI Rationale

Rather than acting as a black box, every recommendation displays transparent bullet points explaining exact reasons why the dish was selected (e.g. "✓ Matches your vegetarian preference", "✓ High protein supports muscle goals").
